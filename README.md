# HelperValley

**English** · [Português (Brasil)](README.pt-BR.md)

A Stardew Valley companion that reads your own save files in the browser. No account, no uploads, no tracking.

**Open it:** [mauricioaires30.github.io/HelperValley](https://mauricioaires30.github.io/HelperValley/)

## Features
- **Local saves, on Windows, Mac and Linux:** pick the saves folder once (`%AppData%\StardewValley\Saves` on Windows, `~/.config/StardewValley/Saves` on Mac and Linux). In Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera, choose "Allow on every visit" and the site always watches it.
- **Live updates:** every time the game saves (when you sleep), the page shows the new day on its own.
- **Remembers your farm:** the last save stays in the browser and opens instantly on your next visit.
- **Multiplayer:** switch between the host and each farmhand.
- **Today:** luck, tomorrow's weather, upcoming birthdays and festivals, and what you can do right now.
- **Calendar:** festivals and birthdays for every season.
- **Relationships:** hearts, gifts left this week, next heart event, and loved gifts you already own.
- **Community Center:** missing items, what is already in your chests, and the Joja route.
- **Museum:** donated, ready to donate and missing items, with where to find each one.
- **Achievements:** progress for each one, like "Mother Catch 99/100".
- **Game art and names**, in English and Portuguese.
